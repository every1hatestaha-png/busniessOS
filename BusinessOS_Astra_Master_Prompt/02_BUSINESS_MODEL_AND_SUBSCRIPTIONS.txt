BUSINESS MODEL AND SUBSCRIPTION SPEC

Commercial model:
- BusinessOS is sold to multiple businesses.
- Each new customer receives a 1-month free trial.
- The trial should feel like the real product, not a crippled demo.
- After the trial, continuing normal transactional use requires active paid access.
- Customers should not need a new installer or separate build when converting from trial to paid.
- Subscription status is controlled centrally.

Recommended concepts:
Customer Account / Workspace
Plan
Subscription
Trial
Entitlement
Feature
Owner Override
Billing Event
Renewal
Suspension
Grace Period

Recommended effective-access logic:
Platform access is allowed when:
- active trial not expired
OR
- active subscription
OR
- valid grace access
OR
- explicit owner temporary override

Access denied/limited when:
- trial expired and no paid entitlement
- subscription expired
- manually suspended

Never erase data at expiry.

Possible plans later:
Starter
Professional
Enterprise
Custom

Do not hardcode commercial pricing into domain logic. Store plans/configuration.

Trial abuse controls to consider later:
- one trial per workspace/customer
- optional verified email/business identity
- owner override
- audit extension history

Manual subscriptions should be possible before automated payment gateway integration.
