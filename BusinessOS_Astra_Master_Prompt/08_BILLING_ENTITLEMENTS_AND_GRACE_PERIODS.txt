BILLING, ENTITLEMENTS, GRACE

Keep billing separate from entitlement.

Billing provider event:
-> updates normalized subscription state
-> entitlement service derives access

This permits:
- manual activation
- offline invoice/business sales
- future Stripe/Paddle/local payment provider/etc.
without rewriting domain enforcement.

Grace period:
optional configurable period after failed/late renewal.

Do not silently grant indefinite grace.

Owner should see:
- past due since
- grace ends
- payment state
- access state

Payment failure must not alter accounting records inside customer BusinessOS operational data.
