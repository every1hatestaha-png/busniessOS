CUSTOMER LIFECYCLE AND EXPIRY

Example lifecycle:

LEAD
-> TRIALING
-> ACTIVE
-> GRACE / PAST_DUE (optional)
-> EXPIRED
-> REACTIVATED or CANCELLED

SUSPENDED is an administrative override and may apply independently.

Trial:
- trialStartAt
- trialEndAt
- createdBy/source
- extension history

Subscription:
- planId
- status
- startsAt
- currentPeriodStart
- currentPeriodEnd
- cancelAtPeriodEnd
- cancelledAt
- provider/customer/subscription references if billing provider is used later

Effective entitlement should be computed from timestamps/status rather than relying solely on a manually updated status field.

Expiry:
- automatic at request time
- optionally synchronized by scheduled job for reporting/notifications
- no data deletion
- transactional writes blocked by central entitlement middleware/service
- read access policy defined explicitly

Suggested customer UX after expiry:
- clearly explain trial/subscription expired
- show renewal/contact action
- keep important historical data readable if policy allows
- block mutation CTAs server-side and UI-side
